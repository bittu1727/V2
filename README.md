# Badam Chhakka - Classic Indian Card Game (Badam Saat / Sevens)

An authentic native Android implementation of **Badam Chhakka** (Badam Saat / Sevens) built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**.

## Features & Highlights

- **Authentic Traditional Rules**:
  - **Mandatory 6♥ Start**: Round opens strictly with the Six of Hearts (Badam Chhakka).
  - **Suits Opening**: Diamonds (♦), Clubs (♣), and Spades (♠) must be opened with their respective 6s.
  - **Bidirectional Sequences**: Cards branch downwards (5 ➔ 4 ➔ 3 ➔ 2 ➔ A) and upwards (7 ➔ 8 ➔ 9 ➔ 10 ➔ J ➔ Q ➔ K).
  - **Signature 5♥ Blocking Rule**: No other suit can play its 5 until the 5 of Hearts has been placed on the felt board.
  - **Traditional Penalty Scoring**: First to shed all cards receives 0 points; opponents take penalty points equal to the total rank value of their leftover cards (A=1, 2-10=face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13).
- **Game Modes**:
  - **Vs Computer**: Offline play with 3 intelligent bots (Kabir, Priya, Rajesh) supporting Casual, Smart, and Master AI difficulty levels.
  - **Pass N Play**: 1-device local multiplayer for 2-4 players with custom names and avatars.
  - **7 Up 7 Down Minigame**: Fast-paced card draw betting minigame (2-6 Down 2x, Lucky 7 5x, 8-K Up 2x).
  - **Daily Lucky Spin & Store**: Spin wheel and coin/gem management.
- **Rich Indian Theme & Felt Customization**:
  - Classic Emerald Felt, Royal Crimson Velvet, Sapphire Blue, and Dark Obsidian tables.
  - Full bilingual support (Hindi / English).
  - Native haptics and procedural sound effects.
